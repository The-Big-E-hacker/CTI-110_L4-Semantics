Overview:

Chelsea FC is a professional football club based in Fulham, West London. Founded in 1905, the club plays its home matches at Stamford Bridge and competes in the Premier League.

  1. Chelsea was founded in 1905.
  2. Chelsea plays at Stamford Bridge.
  3. Chelsea's nickname is the Blues.

About Chelsea

Chelsea Facts

Chelsea's primary team colors are royal blue and white. The team is widely known across international sports as The Blues.

  • Location: London, England
  • Stadium: Stamford Bridge
  • Nickname: The Blues

Fun Fact

Stamford Bridge is one of the oldest grounds in English football history. Chelsea FC has played at this iconic venue continuously since its founding over a century ago.
